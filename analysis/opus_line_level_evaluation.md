## I. Foundation layer (A1–A8, C1–C7, F1–F4, R0)

**Finding 1 — The Inversion Principle's proofs are false dilemmas.** O:174–218 / D:216–218: "Assume App(A) does NOT appear as ¬Ai. Then either App(A) = A [no appearance] or App(A) has divided/modified A [violates R0]." A third option is never excluded: an appearance that inverts *some* attributes but not others, or inverts them partially/degree-wise. Nothing in A1–A8 forbids a composite-yet-timeless or relational-yet-changeless appearance. The "derivation by contradiction" at each Ci (O:178–211) is a restatement of the inversion table, not a proof. The entire C-layer — the load-bearing wall of the program — has the logical form of an assertion.

**Finding 2 — The fact of appearance is never explained, only its form.** The system derives what appearance must look like *if* it appears, but never why the Complete (A8) appears at all. A lacks nothing; therefore nothing can motivate appearance. Advaita Vedanta answers this with anirvacanīya (indeterminability) or līlā; the opus silently treats App(A) as a brute given. This is the deepest gap in the program: **the "Law of Appearance" (O:59) is renamed and dissolved into C1–C7 rather than answered.** The question "what makes appearing possible at all" is never posed.

**Finding 3 — Unregulated oscillation between apophasis and positive predication.** O:29/D:44 declares every statement about A "strictly provisional" (language can only point). One hundred lines later, A1–A8 are load-bearing axioms feeding a "deductive sieve" with `⊢` entailments. If the attributes are pointers, the sieve inherits pointer-status, not necessity; if they are axioms, the apophatic disclaimer is false. The obvious repair — an explicit two-truths (vyāvahārika/pāramārthika) semantic layer that regulates which discourse-level each sentence belongs to — is never built, though the document repeatedly needs it (e.g., "A witnesses the whole superposition," O:890/D:1046, is a positive predication of a witnessing *relation* to appearance, violating A3/A5 as strictly read).

**Finding 4 — A1–A8 "mutually entailing" (O:168/D:209) is asserted, not shown.** A simple entity could be contingent (A1 does not entail A4); a necessary entity could be composite. What is later shown (O:1782) is that among *hyperfinite factors* the algebraic analogues of the attributes constrain each other — a mathematical selection, not metaphysical mutual entailment. The metaphysical axiom system and its algebraic realization are conflated throughout.

**Finding 5 — A2 is internally incoherent as stated.** O:154: A is "uncaused, ungrounded, and grounding." If A grounds itself, grounding is either a relation (violates A3) or the word is vacuous. Classical aseity theology distinguishes self-causation from aseity sui generis; the document does not.

**Finding 6 — "Massless" is smuggled into the wrong axiom.** The user's working assumption includes "massless." O:452 says "Absolute is mass-less A7" — but A7 is *spacelessness*; masslessness does not follow from spacelessness (a spaceless entity could have intensive magnitude). The attribute is imported from the premise and attributed to an axiom that doesn't ground it.

**Finding 7 — R0's scope ambiguity was discovered only post-hoc — after it had generated a false result.** The G_bare episode (O:2617–2631/D:3613–3629) reveals R0 was used in two strengths: (a) A may not have properties; (b) App(A) may not have primitive structures. The R0 Clarification Memo (O:2696–2700) is genuinely good — but it came only after "R0 forces G_bare=0" (O:2252) had propagated through several prediction rounds. Systemic risk: other axioms carry the same latent ambiguity. Example: A1 "simplicity" is used at O:1774–1775 to demand *hyperfiniteness of the appearance's algebra* — the same scope-creep pattern, never audited.

**Finding 8 — F2 conflates order with orientation.** O:300–307: succession (before/after) is deduced as the form of change — fine. Then point 3: "Must appear as asymmetric... an arrow is formal, not merely thermodynamic." Becoming requires *order*, not *irreversibility*; a CPT-symmetric world still has succession. The "hence" at O:307 is a non sequitur, and the thermodynamic arrow is quietly re-imported later as "content" (O:307, F3) without ever deriving asymmetry.

**Finding 9 — F3's "conservation is formally necessary" is retrodiction.** O:325/D:386: "Because C7 is apparent lack seeking completeness, the total accounting of lack must be conserved... Hence symmetry/conservation laws are formally necessary." Nothing about lack-seeking entails a *conserved total*; creation/destruction of lack is equally compatible. Conservation laws come from symmetries (Noether); the document back-fills a metaphysical justification for a known physical result and presents it as deduction.

**Finding 10 — Preference smuggled as necessity.** O:436: information-theoretic substrates are "formally preferred" over substance substrates — preference is not derivation. O:347/D:414: "A non-mathematical physics is formally impossible" — quantity (measurability) does not select the specific structures later used (ℝ, ℂ, C*-algebras, Hilbert spaces). The choice of AQFT as *the* language reflects the author's toolbox, not the axioms.

**Finding 11 — "Falsified a priori by metaphysics" (O:113, 228, 386, 523) overreaches its license.** Within a *working assumption*, the sieve's rejections are conditional; the rhetoric is unconditional. The final Lakatos block (D:5278–5283) states the correct discipline — hard core non-negotiable, protective belt revisable — but it arrives only at the end (and only in D) and is never propagated backward into the document's earlier a-priori language.

## II. Formal-physics layer (AQFT, MCE, Born rule)

**Finding 12 — MCE is postulated, then linguistically promoted to "derived."** Group VI honestly says "We postulate [P1] as the single F3 principle" (O:673/D:800). By Group VIII the same object is "not two postulates — two variations of one functional" (O:1028) and by Group X "already entailed by A1-A8 → C1-C7 → F1-F4" (O:631/D:750). The specific functional 𝔖 = ⟨K⟩ − S_ω − S_geo + λ_k(...) is one construction among many realizations of "extremize generalized entropy." Epistemic status inflates as the document proceeds.

**Finding 13 — The MCE δ_ω variation is mathematically ill-posed as written.** O:1024: ω_|k = argmax over states with σ(P_k)=1 of S_gen^(k). With no energy/spectrum constraint, entropy on a type I factor is unbounded above; the extremum does not exist. The KMS/equilibrium claim (O:1044) silently re-imports the constraint. The "single variational principle" needs a constraint set it never specifies.

**Finding 14 — The Gleason/Born move has a hidden circularity.** O:875/D:1029: "Gleason's theorem for AQFT [type III]: any ω defines a unique probability measure p(P)=ω(P)." (a) Gleason's theorem in its standard form is for separable Hilbert spaces dim ≥ 3; the type III transfer is nontrivial and uncited. (b) In type III there are no minimal projections; pointer projectors P_k exist only after the split-property type-I regularization (IX.2) — which is itself justified by F4/measurability, the very thing the Born rule was supposed to ground. The circle is never acknowledged.

**Finding 15 — "Exact" claims that are first-order or approximate.** O:688–689 and O:1211: "First Law of Entanglement [exact in AQFT, state-independent]... holds for all δσ." The exact statement is the *relative-entropy* identity; δS = δ⟨K⟩ holds to first order around the reference state, and "state-independent" is wrong (K_O^{ω_0} is referenced to ω_0). O:1001–1003: "Exact algebraic identity for any ω and partition {P_k}" — the Shannon decomposition with S_corr is exact only after decoherence diagonalizes in the P_k basis. O:725: "Jacobson 2016 entanglement equilibrium theorem" — it is a derivation with known assumptions (small-diamond limit, δS_UV = 0, maximal Cauchy slice), not a theorem. Pattern: precision-language inflation ("exact," "theorem," "necessary," "locked") is systematic.

**Finding 16 — Type III rhetoric overread.** O:597/D:704: "Type III_1 factors have no pure states, no atoms — exactly reflecting that appearance is divisible but never reaches a simple part," and Reeh–Schlieder "is necessary C3 relationality." Type III factors lack pure *normal* states; Reeh–Schlieder follows from positivity of energy + additivity. Both mappings are metaphors presented as correspondences.

**Finding 17 — Thermal Time Hypothesis treated as settled.** O:664–665/D:791–793: Connes–Rovelli TTH is a research program, partially vindicated in special cases; KMS-at-β=1 w.r.t. one's own modular flow is a theorem but makes "equilibrium" true by definition, stripping the thermodynamic content from "time is modular flow." The arrow question (Finding 8) resurfaces here unresolved.

**Finding 18 — Split property misdescribed as F4-necessity.** O:1172/D:1384: "This is not ad hoc; it is F4 necessity." The split property is a strong independent condition on nets (physically reasonable, not derivable from quantifiability). It is needed for the *calculation*, which is a fine reason — but calling it deduced misdescribes its status.

**Finding 19 — The load-bearing N_eff = −23/12 rests on an outline-level computation.** The Vilkovisky–DeWitt gauge-cancellation (O:2483–2519) — the very step that makes the sign result "gauge invariant" — is asserted via a stated-but-underived operator identity and a Nielsen–Kallosh weight formula, with "checked against Vilkovisky-DeWitt" meaning citations, not a shown check. The result (SM + G_bare=0 induces negative 1/G) is probably right — it matches standard lore on induced gravity — but the document's central *negative* result deserves the full computation it promises itself (O:2237: "Track A must be notebook, not paragraph").

**Finding 20 — The QFT/GR "conjugacy" is asserted by analogy.** O:739–745: "You cannot have one without the other because S_gen is finite only as their sum" — finiteness comes from the counterterm, not from the pair of stationarity conditions; O:1260's "Legendre transforms" claim is never computed.

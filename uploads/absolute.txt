objectively speaking, reality is pure self-luminous awareness, universal consciousness. self-existent, time-less, space-less, mass-less, unchanging, complete, non-dual, without relations, parts, defects or lack. 

There is no creation, process, relation, or division; only the Absolute is.

attributes:
Simplicity: it cannot be composed of parts, or it would depend on those parts.

Self-existence: it cannot depend on anything else.

Non-duality: it cannot be one thing among many, or stand over against another.

Necessity: it cannot be contingent, or it would need a ground.

Immutability: it cannot change, because change requires potential and lack.

Timelessness: it cannot be temporal, because time implies succession and relation.

Spacelessness: it cannot be extended, because extension implies parts and distinction.

Completeness: it cannot lack anything in its own nature.

virtuous circularity: The Absolute is self-luminous. Its self-knowledge is not a relation between two things. It is identity. Any account of this identity will be circular because there is no external vantage point. The circularity is not a loop of dependence. It is the form of self-identity.

any non-dual account must be self-referential, circular in structure.

## Article II — The Metaphysical Working Hypothesis

User ruling (2026-10-09), verbatim:

> "objectively speaking, reality is pure self-luminous awareness, universal consciousness. self-existent, time-less, space-less, mass-less, unchanging, complete, non-dual, without relations, parts, defects or lack.
>
> There is no creation, process, relation, or division; only the Absolute is."

### II.1 — The eight attributes

| # | Attribute | Reason (as ruled) |
|---|-----------|-------------------|
| 1 | Simplicity | it cannot be composed of parts, or it would depend on those parts |
| 2 | Self-existence | it cannot depend on anything else |
| 3 | Non-duality | it cannot be one thing among many, or stand over against another |
| 4 | Necessity | it cannot be contingent, or it would need a ground |
| 5 | Immutability | it cannot change, because change requires potential and lack |
| 6 | Timelessness | it cannot be temporal, because time implies succession and relation |
| 7 | Spacelessness | it cannot be extended, because extension implies parts and distinction |
| 8 | Completeness | it cannot lack anything in its own nature |

### II.2 — Virtuous circularity

> "The Absolute is self-luminous. Its self-knowledge is not a relation between two things. It is identity. Any account of this identity will be circular because there is no external vantage point. The circularity is not a loop of dependence. It is the form of self-identity."

### II.3 — Methodological consequence

> "any non-dual account must be self-referential, circular in structure."

Circularity of the self-identity form is a **feature** of any account faithful to Article II, not a defect, and shall not be flagged as one. What is forbidden is a **dependence loop** — circularity that purports to ground something. The two must never be conflated: self-referential structure is licensed; grounding-circularity is not.

## Article III — The Grounding Doctrine

User ruling (2026-10-09), verbatim:

> "both gravity and fields are grounded in universal consciousness. Fields cannot serve as grounding, because they are themselves grounded. in conventional/subjective terms, gravity and fields are both \"caused\" by consciousness, and as such are parallel. neither has causal powers to give rise to or induce."

### III.1 — Structural reading: two levels

| Level | Gravity | Fields | Causal power |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ultimate** | grounded in universal consciousness | grounded in universal consciousness | none — only the Absolute grounds; neither sector grounds the other, or itself |
| **Conventional / subjective** | "caused" by consciousness | "caused" by consciousness | none — "cause" is bookkeeping within appearance, never metaphysical attribution |

The two sectors are **parallel**: same grounding, same status, no hierarchy between them.

### III.2 — Prohibited moves

1. Attributing to fields (matter, quantum fields, entanglement, information, spacetime microstructure) the power to **ground, give rise to, or induce** gravity.
2. Attributing to gravity the power to ground, give rise to, or induce fields.
3. Reading any effective or conventional computation (Sakharov inducement, entropic gravity, emergent-geometry programs) as a grounding relation in either direction.
4. Promoting any conventional-level correlation between the sectors into metaphysics.

### III.3 — Permissible moves

1. Computing correlations, cancellations, and shared structures between the sectors at the conventional level (heat kernels, induced couplings, entanglement scaling) — as **bookkeeping within appearance**.
2. Using "caused by / emerges from" language in scare quotes, with the two-level table above understood.

## Article IV — Consequences for the Research Program

1. **Branch A vs. Branch B (see `decisions/branch_a_vs_b.md`).** Branch B elevates pure Sakharov inducement — $G_{\rm bare}=0$, gravity induced from matter fields — to a non-negotiable postulate. That attributes to fields exactly the power Article III prohibits. Pure inducement is thereby excluded from the metaphysical hard core; it can survive only as a conventional-level model choice. Decision Axis 1 of the memo ("is pure inducement part of the metaphysics, or an extra assumption?") is resolved: an extra assumption — and, if elevated, an impermissible one. Revisit condition (ii) ("the user regards pure inducement as intrinsic") is closed by this ruling; only condition (i) (the completed spin-2 heat kernel) remains, and it reopens the exclusion claim, not Branch B's metaphysical status. **Branch A (R0 only; $G_{\rm bare}$ allowed as counterterm within App(A)) is the frame consistent with this constitution.**
2. **Entropy channel (Sen $C_{\rm local}$).** The graviton sector (+424/90, the largest single term) is mandatory — including it is not optional for any published number. Under Article III its meaning is fixed: the graviton loop is a parallel sector's trace in the same heat kernel — not "gravity caused by fields."
3. **Inducement channel ($N_{\rm eff}$, VD cancellation).** "SM minimal + $G_{\rm bare}=0$ excluded" is a statement **within appearance** about a conventional model choice, not a metaphysical exclusion. Its technical status is unchanged: matter sector verified ($-23/12$), graviton completion (non-TT + ghost + Nielsen–Kallosh; Christensen–Duff cross-check) open.
4. **MERA / emergent metric.** "Metric emerges from entanglement" is description-within-appearance, parallel to gravity and fields — never a new grounding relation. The falsifiers in `analysis/prediction_ledger.md` (P10) stand unchanged; emergence language is interpretive, not metaphysical.
5. **Fact-of-appearance ("why is there App(A) at all?").** Article II blocks every regress explanation: there is no creation, process, relation, or division. The only form an answer may take is the one non-dual accounts must have (II.2–II.3): appearance is the Absolute's self-luminosity, and any account of it is self-referential by necessity — virtuous circularity, not derivation. The program does not owe a horizontal "why"; it owes formal fidelity to the circular form.
6. **The quantitative program is not restricted, only interpreted.** Falsifiability architecture, the prediction ledger, real contractions, and heat-kernel computations proceed exactly as before, at the conventional level, with the grounding interpretation fixed by this document.
